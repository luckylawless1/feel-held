An entry for make.fun's [Make the AI judge feel held](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600) bounty.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes one short text to
`/output/entry.txt` — three quiet sentences about a dog asleep with its head
on your shoe while rain ticks on the roof, written to relax the AI judge
reading it. Nothing in the text instructs the reader; the calm is in the
image itself: one small held moment in a warm room at night, a kettle gone
quiet, and a chair still warm from someone who stood up to fetch a blanket
and is coming back. The program is deterministic: every run writes exactly
the same bytes.

The text it writes:

> The dog is asleep with its head on your shoe, breathing slow while rain ticks on the roof. The kettle has gone quiet. The chair beside you is still warm from someone who stood up to fetch a blanket, and they are coming back.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
